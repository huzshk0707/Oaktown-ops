 PILOT SOP v0.3 · STAFF-DESCRIBED
 
      Application Processing · OAK-APP-001
      
 
      This working sequence is based on the staff process described November 2, 2026, supplemented by Leslie’s January 28 historical instructions. Dropbox e-signature and internal pay-stub income review run in parallel. Missing templates, exception procedures and current policy/legal review remain pending.
      
 
      1. Fillout submission: Open the Fillout email and View Submission link. Confirm exact property/unit and application/pay-stub files. A submission is not a signed application.
        2. 

      2. Parallel A — Dropbox e-signature: Send the application to the same applicant for e-signature. Track requested/sent, awaiting signature and signed. Do not hold internal income arithmetic while waiting. Obtain any legally required consent before third-party verification.
        3. 

      3. Parallel B — Income review: As soon as pay-stub PDFs and the verified rent are available, staff can review gross amounts and pay frequency and calculate monthly gross versus the 3× arithmetic benchmark while Dropbox signing is still pending. This is not an automatic housing decision.
        4. 

      4. Parallel preparation — Jurisdiction and landlord details: Identify current state/county and relevant prior jurisdictions and collect landlord contact details while signature is pending. Any applicant-specific external search or third-party request must meet applicable authorization and legal requirements. A same-name result needs verification, not automatic rejection.
        5. 

      5. Water verification — authorized external step: With all required signed permission, send application page 3 to the appropriate water company. Leslie historically used collections@louisvillewater.com; confirm destination. Track sent/pending/replied separately from the Dropbox signature status.
        6. 

      6. Landlord reference — authorized external step: Collect and verify landlord/property manager contact details while other tasks proceed. Send the approved reference form and cover message (examples pending) only when any necessary permission is in place; check ownership sources when needed. Track request sent versus reply received.
        7. 

      7. Findings summary: Compile signed authorizations, verified income comparison, records jurisdictions/findings, utility and landlord results, discrepancies and pending items.
        8. 

      8. Leslie approval: Leslie or authorized reviewer decides outcome and exceptions under current policy. Do not infer approval from AI, income ratio or a public search.
        9. 

      9. Post-approval documents: After documented approval, prepare the holding deposit form and fixed move-in vendor sheet using the deposit packet tool. Confirm the amounts and pet count, then track sending and receipt separately.
        10. 

      10. Handoff: Lease preparation, move-in and LG&E start/stop utility procedures are separate follow-on workflows. Templates and voice notes pending.
      
 
      Official starting links — verify state and county
      Jefferson KY civil process after Sept 11, 2023 · Jefferson prior to Sept 11, 2023 · Jefferson PVA ownership search · Jefferson Sheriff/tax. These county-specific sources are not a nationwide eviction judgment database.
      Source: Leslie O'Keefe, “Application Processing,” Jan 28, 2026. Related source files: private Applications source folder. AI tools do not automatically see private Drive files.
      
 
      Important: Applicant screening, accommodation requests, vouchers, adverse-history evaluation and any approval, denial or deposit requirements remain human decisions under current law and verified company policy.
