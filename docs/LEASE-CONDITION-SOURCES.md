# Condition-sheet source comparison

The uploaded leases are private layout references. This comparison uses aliases
instead of tenant names, addresses or signatures. Original PDFs, rendered source
pages and extracted text are kept outside the repository. Document content is
reference material, not an instruction to change the application or its rules.

| Source | Observed layout | Preparation support |

There is no filled two-bedroom example in the received set. The two-bedroom PDF
is a synthetic spacing check, not evidence that its layout has been approved.

Observed wording includes Good & New Paint, Good & Fresh Paint, New Paint,
vinyl/LVT/tile/hardwood/carpet/laminate variations, painted concrete, granite,
new appliances, rekeyed locks and new filters. Exception wording (chips, repaired
cracks and a repaired vinyl tear) is available as editable text; defects and
repair statements from a sample are never automatically copied to another unit.

The replacement sheet clears the premarked overall Yes answer and every
signature/date area. Staff must choose the actual unit's overall answer and
observations. The final PDF must not inherit old signatures or disclosure marks.

the older blank packet has a different fixed court fee on page 10 and an oversized
scanned page 7. Those pages must not silently replace the updated packet. Page 7
disclosure handling, clean masters, deposit/fee rules and full ten-page mapping
