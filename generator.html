Firm Mailing Book: US Letter landscape (11 × 8.5 in), 100% scale, headers/footers off, with original scale. The sheet artwork reproduces the supplied PS Form 3877 sheet. Confirm with the accepting Post Office whether its form-approval requirements apply to this computer-generated printout.
